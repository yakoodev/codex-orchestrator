# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on technical arguments, not personalities.
- Assume good intent and provide clear context when disagreeing.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Deliberate leaking of sensitive information.
- Repeated disruptive behavior in issues/PRs.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject content that violates this policy.
